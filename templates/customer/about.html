🍔 Our story

Food deserves to arrive happy

FOODIES is an original food-delivery platform built for local kitchens. We connect hungry people with the restaurants they love, keep pricing honest and give every partner — restaurant owners and delivery riders — the tools to grow.

Our mission

Make great local food accessible within minutes, with transparent pricing and reliable delivery for every order.

Four interfaces, one platform

Customers, restaurant owners, delivery partners and administrators each get a purpose-built dashboard on the same Django backend and database.

Built responsibly

Server-side pricing, role-based permissions, hashed passwords and real database transactions on every checkout.

How FOODIES works

Step 1

Browse & discover

Filter by cuisine, rating, delivery time or pure-veg and see only approved kitchens.

Step 2

Add to cart

Your cart lives in our database — prices, taxes and coupons are always recalculated on the server.

Step 3

Restaurant cooks

The kitchen accepts your order, prepares it and marks it ready for pickup.

Step 4

Track to your door

A delivery partner is assigned and you can follow every status change live.
