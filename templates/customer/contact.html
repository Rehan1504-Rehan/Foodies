Frequently asked

How do I track my order?

Open My Orders → Track. The page reads the live status from our database and refreshes automatically.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes — until the order is picked up by a delivery partner. Cancelling from the order page updates the database instantly.

How do coupons work?

Apply a code in your cart. Minimum order, expiry and maximum discount are validated on the server before the discount is applied.

When can I review a restaurant?

Only after an order has actually been delivered to you — that keeps ratings honest.

Which payments are supported?

Cash on delivery always, plus online payments through Razorpay when the gateway keys are configured.
